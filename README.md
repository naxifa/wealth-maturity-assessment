# Wealth Management Digital & AI Maturity Self-Assessment Tool

An interactive diagnostic instrument designed to evaluate digital and artificial intelligence maturity across wealth management firms and Independent Broker-Dealers (IBDs).

### Industry Calibration & Anchor
* **Target Industry:** Wealth Management & Independent Broker-Dealers (IBDs)
* **Calibration Benchmark:** Raymond James Financial (~Stage 3.2 Median)

### Core Capability Dimensions
1. **Custodial Data Architecture & Interoperability:** Measures aggregation, normalization, and real-time flow of client, tax-lot, and clearing records.
2. **Omnichannel Client Acquisition & Onboarding:** Assesses end-to-end client friction across biometric KYC/AML, digital account opening, and synchronized portal engagement.
3. **Automated Portfolio Engineering & Rebalancing:** Evaluates drift monitoring, automated trade batch routing, and algorithmic tax-loss harvesting.
4. **Advisory AI, Copilots & Predictive Intelligence:** Assesses governed CRM copilots, pre-meeting briefing automation, and predictive client-retention intelligence.
5. **Supervisory Governance, Compliance Agility & AI Fluency:** Evaluates real-time NLP communications surveillance, AI Governance Board oversight, and workforce prompt fluency.

### Architecture & Technical Stack
* Single-page responsive client application built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+).
* Zero-dependency backend; scoring algorithms and multi-dimensional gap evaluations execute entirely client-side.
* Dynamic multi-axis radar visualizations rendered via Chart.js.
